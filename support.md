## FAQ

**Where are my clips saved?**
In your Photos, inside the "Splitly" album.

**Why is the last clip shorter?**
It contains the remaining time. For example, 130s split at 60s gives 60s, 60s and 10s.

**The app can't save my clips.**
Go to Settings → Splitly → Photos and allow "Add Photos Only".

**Are my videos uploaded anywhere?**
No. Everything is processed on your device. [Privacy Policy](privacy)
