# Splitly – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 28, 2026

Splitly ("the App") is developed by **Clixasoft**. This policy explains how the App handles your information. In short, the App does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

## Information We Collect
We do not collect any personal information. The App has no accounts or sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and it does not send any data to our servers or to third parties.

## Photos & Videos
- **Selecting videos:** You choose a video using your device's built-in system photo picker. The App can only access the video you select. It cannot see the rest of your library.
- **Saving clips:** The App asks for permission to add items to your photo library. It uses this permission only to save the clips it creates, into an album named "Splitly".
- **Processing:** All video processing happens entirely on your device. Your videos are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Temporary files:** While processing, the App creates temporary files on your device. It deletes them automatically once processing is finished.

## Internet Access
The App works fully offline and makes no network requests for its core functionality.

## Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party services that collect data.

## Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Your Choices
You can change or revoke the App's photo library permission at any time in your device settings:
- **iOS:** Settings → Splitly → Photos
- **Android:** Settings → Apps → Splitly → Permissions

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
