# Splitly

**Split long videos into perfect short clips.**

Pick a video, choose the clip length, and tap Split. Splitly cuts your video into equal clips and saves them straight to your Photos, in order.

## Features

- **Exact splitting:** a 130-second video at 60 seconds gives 60s, 60s and 10s. Nothing is lost.
- **Live preview:** see how many clips you'll get before you start.
- **Original quality:** keeps resolution, orientation and sound. Supports 4K and HDR.
- **Quick presets:** 10s, 15s, 30s, 60s, 90s and 3 minutes, or any custom length.
- **Auto save:** clips go to the "Splitly" album in Photos.
- **Private:** everything runs on your device. No account, no uploads, no ads, no tracking.
